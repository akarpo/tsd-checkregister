## Outcome

**Data.** Ingested the Mar, Apr and May 2026 registers (approved 2026-05-19,
2026-06-16, 2026-07-22): +2,799 lines / +$50,768,973.41 → **227,066 lines /
$1,277,793,488.14**. FY26 complete through May 2026.

**Schema.** Collapsed the dual `Fiscal Year` (approving meeting) / `Issue Date FY`
columns into a single `Fiscal Year` derived from the issue date on the Michigan
Jul 1 – Jun 30 boundary — new `build/fiscal_year.py`. Workbook 8 sheets → 7
(`By Issue-Date FY x Fund` became redundant). Forced by the May 2026 register,
which would otherwise have opened an FY27 bucket holding only FY26 money.

**Defects fixed.**
1. `parser.py` capped budget units at `\d{3,15}`, silently dropping 16-digit
   capital-projects lines (fund 423) — the Apr 2026 register was short exactly
   $35,188.11. Widened to `\d{3,20}`.
2. `write_dashboard` ran a blanket `re.sub(r'FY\d\d-FY\d\d', ...)` over the whole
   page; the 2026-06-02 rebuild had overwritten 8 hand-written adoption-cycle
   spans in the curriculum narrative. Anchored to the five real site-wide labels;
   originals restored from `13169de`.
3. Three issue dates are wrong in the source PDFs themselves — repaired via a
   documented `DATE_CORRECTIONS` override rather than by editing output, so a
   re-parse cannot reintroduce them.
4. Dashboard source link pointed at `/mi/troymi/` (Troy city) instead of
   `/mi/troysd/`; the "through &lt;month&gt;" caption now derives from the data.
5. Five figures left over from the original FY23-FY26 build were still on the
   page and contradicted the header beside them: the "All disbursements" badge
   read $417M, the methodology sentence read "$203M + $214M = $417M", the FY
   cards were labelled "% of 4-year total" across 16 years, the "Total spending"
   tile said "4 fiscal years", and the FY26 caption said "through April 2026".
   The percentages themselves were computed against the real grand total, so
   that one was a labelling error rather than a maths error.
6. **The curriculum spotlight ran on hard-coded per-FY anchors.**
   `split_curriculum_pd_v2.py` applied a computed PD percentage to fixed totals
   for three of the four swimlanes and printed JS arrays to paste into
   `index.html`. Because the totals never moved, the filters behind them were
   never checked against their own output. Deriving them exposed two real bugs:
   the 6-12 Math filter matched `LARSON` to catch Ron Larson's Big Ideas texts
   and swept in **$8.4M** of construction, utilities and paving for Larson
   *School* ($4.7M of Barton Malow alone); and Illustrative Math, which ships
   through Imagine Learning, was caught only for the rows whose truncated
   description read `ILLUSTRATIVE` — $44K of $748K. A third issue: 60 rows
   matched both grade bands of one subject (nearly all function-code 221, which
   is grade-neutral) and were double-counted when lanes were summed.

**Nothing hand-maintained.** `curriculum.py` and `coverage.py` now derive the
swimlanes, period totals, key takeaways and coverage tables; both travel in the
dashboard payload and the page renders from it. Editorial wording stays authored,
but every number inside it interpolates. Published curriculum totals moved as a
result — K-5 ELA $3.75M → $3.38M, K-5 Math $3.46M → $3.11M, 6-12 Math $1.69M →
$1.31M, combined $10.46M → $9.30M — and are now reproducible from `rebuild.py`.
